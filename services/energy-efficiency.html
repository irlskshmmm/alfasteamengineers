    Energy Efficiency

    Practical measures to reduce fuel, steam and water consumption — identified, quantified and implemented.

    In most steam plants the cheapest unit of energy is the one you stop wasting. We identify efficiency measures, quantify them against your own operating data, and support implementation so the savings are actually realised.

    Scope of work

    • Heat recovery

      Flash steam, blowdown heat and flue gas recovery opportunities.

    • Condensate return improvement

      Increasing return rates to cut fuel, water and treatment chemical use.

    • Trap and leak management

      Programmes to find and fix steam losses and keep them fixed.

    • Insulation upgrades

      Specification of insulation for lines, valves and fittings with payback estimates.

    • Combustion optimisation

      Air–fuel ratio, excess air and burner tuning recommendations.

    • Monitoring & targeting

      Metering and KPIs so consumption can be tracked after improvements are made.

    When to call us

    • Fuel cost is a large share of operating cost
    • Visible steam leaks or plumes from vents
    • Low condensate return to the boiler house
    • Sustainability or emissions targets to meet

    What you receive

    • Energy-saving measures with estimated payback
    • Technical specifications for selected measures
    • Implementation and vendor coordination support
    • Post-implementation verification
