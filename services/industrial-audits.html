Service

Industrial Audits

Structured, on-site assessment of steam, boiler and utility systems with clear, prioritised findings.

An audit turns walk-down observations and measurements into decisions. We assess how your steam and utility systems actually operate, document where losses and risks sit, and give you a prioritised action list you can budget against.

Scope of work

  • Steam system audit

    Generation, distribution, end use and condensate return assessed as one system.

  • Steam trap survey

    Trap-by-trap inspection with status, application suitability and replacement recommendations.

  • Boiler performance assessment

    Efficiency evaluation from fuel, flue gas, feed water and blowdown observations.

  • Insulation survey

    Identification of bare or damaged insulation on lines, valves and flanges.

  • Safety & reliability review

    Observations on safety valves, drainage, water hammer risks and maintenance practices.

  • Utility metering review

    Assessment of fuel, steam, water and condensate measurement for ongoing monitoring.

When to call us

  • No clear picture of where steam is being used or lost
  • Preparing an energy-saving or capex plan
  • Recurring breakdowns without a clear root cause
  • Management or compliance review coming up

What you receive

  • Written audit report with findings and photographs
  • Prioritised list of recommendations
  • Estimated savings and implementation cost where measurable
  • Follow-up review on request
