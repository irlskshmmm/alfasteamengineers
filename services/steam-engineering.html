    Steam Engineering

    Design, review and optimisation of steam generation, distribution and condensate recovery systems.

    A steam system is only as good as its weakest link — an undersized line, a failed trap, a missing separator. We look at the whole loop, from boiler outlet to condensate return, and engineer it to deliver dry steam at the right pressure where your process needs it.

    Scope of work

    • Steam distribution design

      Line sizing, header layout, drainage points, expansion and venting for new and modified networks.

    • Pressure reduction & control

      Selection and layout of PRV stations, control valves and safety relief for each process user.

    • Steam trapping

      Trap type selection by application, trap station layout and trap survey programmes.

    • Condensate recovery

      Return line design, flash steam recovery and pumping solutions to bring heat back to the boiler house.

    • Steam quality

      Separators, strainers and air venting to improve dryness and protect heat transfer surfaces.

    • Process heating applications

      Heat exchangers, jacketed vessels, dryers and tracing — sized for stable, efficient operation.

    When to call us

    • Wet steam, water hammer or slow process heat-up
    • Condensate being drained to waste
    • Plant expansion or new process equipment planned
    • Frequent trap, valve or gasket failures

    What you receive

    • Engineering drawings and P&IDs
    • Line and equipment sizing calculations
    • Bill of materials and specifications
    • Installation and commissioning guidance
