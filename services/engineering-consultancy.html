Service

Engineering Consultancy

Independent technical advice for projects, procurement and plant problems — from concept to commissioning.

Sometimes you need an experienced engineer on your side of the table. We provide independent consultancy for steam and thermal utility projects, technical evaluation of vendor offers, and troubleshooting when something on the plant is not working as it should.

Scope of work

  • Project engineering

    Concept, basic and detailed engineering for steam and thermal utility packages.

  • Technical specifications

    Equipment and material specifications for tendering and purchase.

  • Bid evaluation

    Technical comparison of vendor offers against your specification.

  • Troubleshooting

    Root-cause investigation of process heating, water hammer, pressure and capacity issues.

  • Installation supervision

    Site checks during erection, testing and commissioning.

  • Component sourcing

    Identifying and supplying the right valves, traps, instruments and fittings for the job.

When to call us

  • A new steam or utility project at concept stage
  • Several vendor quotes that are hard to compare
  • A plant problem that keeps coming back
  • No in-house steam specialist

What you receive

  • Technical reports and recommendations
  • Specifications and bid comparison sheets
  • Site visit reports
  • Commissioning checklists
