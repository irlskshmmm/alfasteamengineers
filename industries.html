Industries

Industries that run on steam

Steam is the workhorse of process heating. Wherever it is generated and used, the same engineering principles decide cost, quality and safety.

  • Food & Beverage

    Cooking, pasteurising, CIP and sterilisation.

  • Dairy

    Pasteurisers, evaporators and cleaning systems.

  • Pharmaceuticals

    Process vessels, sterilisers and clean utilities.

  • Chemicals & Petrochemicals

    Reactors, reboilers, tracing and tank heating.

  • Textiles

    Dyeing, drying, stentering and finishing.

  • Paper & Packaging

    Drying cylinders, corrugators and heating.

  • Sugar & Distilleries

    Evaporation, distillation and process heating.

  • Rubber & Tyre

    Curing presses and vulcanisers.

  • Hospitals & Hospitality

    Laundries, kitchens, sterilisation and hot water.

  • Edible Oil & Solvent Extraction

    Refining, deodorising and extraction plants.
