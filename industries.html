    Industries that run on steam

    Steam is the workhorse of process heating. Wherever it is generated and used, the same engineering principles decide cost, quality and safety.

    • 02 Oil & Gas
    • 03 Chemical Processing
    • 04 Pharmaceuticals

    • 06 Pulp & Paper
    • 07 Manufacturing
    • 08 Textiles
    • 09 Sugar & Distilleries
    • 10 Dairy

    • Power Plants

      Boiler houses, auxiliary steam and condensate systems.

    • Agro & Food Processing

      Cooking, drying, pasteurising, CIP and sterilisation.

    • Pharmaceuticals & Chemicals

      Process vessels, reactors, sterilisers and clean utilities.

    • Manufacturing & Engineering

      Process heating, curing, drying and plant utilities.

    • Oil & Gas

      Tracing, tank heating, reboilers and utility steam.

    Also serving

    • Dairy

      Pasteurisers, evaporators and cleaning systems.

    • Textiles

      Dyeing, drying, stentering and finishing.

    • Paper & Packaging

      Drying cylinders, corrugators and heating.

    • Sugar & Distilleries

      Evaporation, distillation and process heating.

    • Rubber & Tyre

      Curing presses and vulcanisers.

    • Hospitals & Hospitality

      Laundries, kitchens, sterilisation and hot water.

    • Edible Oil & Solvent Extraction

      Refining, deodorising and extraction plants.
