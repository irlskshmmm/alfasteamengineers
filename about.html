About us

Engineering partners for steam-dependent plants

Alfa Steam Engineers & Consultants helps industrial plants generate, distribute and use steam better — with practical engineering, honest assessment and hands-on support.

Who we are

Alfa Steam Engineers & Consultants is an engineering and consultancy firm focused on steam and thermal utility systems. We work with plant owners, utility managers and project teams who need steam to be available, efficient and safe.

Our work spans the full steam loop — boilers and boiler house auxiliaries, distribution networks, process heating equipment, steam traps and condensate recovery. Because these parts affect one another, we look at them as one system rather than in isolation.

Alongside engineering and audits, we help clients specify and source the components our recommendations call for, so there is a straight line from finding to fix.

What clients can expect

  • Evidence first

    Recommendations based on site observations and your operating data — not assumptions.

  • Safety in every decision

    Pressure systems demand care. Safe operation is designed in, not added later.

  • Clear documentation

    Reports, drawings and specifications your team can act on and keep.

  • Practical implementation

    Solutions that fit your plant, your budget and your shutdown windows.

Our approach

How an engagement runs

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us about your plant, the problem or the project. We respond with the questions and data we need.

  2. 02

    Site study

    Our engineers walk the system, take observations and review operating data with your team.

  3. 03

    Analysis & report

    We engineer the solution and present clear, prioritised recommendations.

  4. 04

    Implementation support

    Specifications, components, supervision and verification until the result is delivered.
